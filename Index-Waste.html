Minimizing Waste

Part of my physical handouts involved printing and assembling a small reference booklet on pharmacology.

However, during initial drafting tests, both that booklet asset and my original research poster structure generated an excess amount of blank scrap paper waste because our trimmed designs did not consume full physical page layouts.

In order to actively combat this footprint, I carefully redesigned our assembly workflow, ensuring that every single part of each paper sheet was fully recycled, optimized, and utilized for an entirely new project element.
