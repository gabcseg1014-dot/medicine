Evolution of Medicine

What is Pharmacology?

Pharmacology is the comprehensive scientific study of the uses, systemic biological effects, and specific modes of action drugs within living organisms.

Why do I Want to be a Pharmacologist?

I want to become a pharmacologist because I love studying medicine, but I prefer minimal patient interactions. Choosing this career path allows me to focus deeply on pure research breakthroughs.

My favorite school subjects have always centered around science—specifically biology and chemistry. I am continuously fascinated by discovering how our biological systems function, and how targeted therapeutics interact with our bodies to cure illnesses!

How Long Does It Take To Become One?

It typically takes about 8 years of intensive post-secondary education to become a professional pharmacologist. This trajectory generally requires obtaining a PhD in pharmacology or a closely related biomedical science branch.

Degrees in pharmacology are primarily offered exclusively at the graduate school level due to how exceptionally broad, fast-moving, and complex the field is. Advanced schooling is also critical to understand regulatory safety frameworks, as developing compound formulations can accidentally become highly toxic if handled without expert clinical standards.
