Process of Deciding my Product

First of all, I knew I had to decide on a topic for my project. Once I decided to focus it on pharmacology, I had to determine a feasible product structure. At first, I encountered layout blocks with this phase, but when I met with my project supervisor, we determined that I would begin by studying Organ-on-a-Chip (OOAC) biomimetic models.

After examining two foundational research papers, my supervisor connected me with an external expert who was willing to guide our development course: Dr. Goodman from UT Southwestern. In the month of November, we met with him over Zoom to gather technical guidance. This consultative meeting helped solidify our strategy, steering our efforts toward creating a comprehensive academic research poster highlighting the historical evolution of medicine.

Handouts: What Does it Mean?

The companion kits serve as a clear physical metaphor tracking the evolution of therapeutic medicine over time. In earlier historical periods, society did not possess quick access to localized adhesive band-aids like we rely on today.
